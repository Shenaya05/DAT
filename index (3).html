About me

I’m Edith Austria, a 3rd-year student who loves turning ideas into designs people can actually use. From app screens to posters and videos, I like making things that look good and make sense at first glance.

Outside of school, I enjoy music, movies and series, exploring new places, and spending time with the people I care about. I’m always learning something new, and I treat every mistake as a step toward getting better.
